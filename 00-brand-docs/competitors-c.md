# Competitor Research: Jean Ivan (Jeanfx) — SMC/ICT Forex & Futures Prop-Firm Education Market (checked 14 Oct 2026)

Jean's best opening is an honest, low-hype offer that is checked against prop-firm rules: "pass and keep the account". Nearly every competitor sells the same thing ("get funded", payout screenshots, lifestyle, an "algorithmic" model with a brand name). Almost none offers a verified track record or firm vetting that isn't tied to an affiliate deal. None serves the burned African and Nigerian prop trader at a mid-ticket price.

## TL;DR

- **The market is saturated with "Get funded" promises and self-reported payouts.** Akram Errachidi (\$150/mo "Matrix Model" NQ Discord), Nick Graves ("Get Funded. Stay Funded.", \$350K+ in self-reported student payouts) and JJ Simon ("\$1,300,000 in prop firm payouts", price only on a call) all lead with payout totals, and none of these totals is independently verified. Payout proof and lifestyle proof are the most commoditised and most distrusted claims in this market, which matches Deep Research B.

- **The price map has three bands, and Jean has an empty middle.** Bottom: free bootcamps, \$45–\$150/mo Discords, ₦150k Nigerian academies. Middle: \$330/mo coaching, \$1,000 lifetime access, \$1,500 1:1 packages. Top: \$4,000–\$4,997 mentorships, or prices only given on a call. Jean's \$399/\$899 one-off tiers have no recurring revenue and no high-ticket rung. Getting to \$50k/mo means adding an application-plus-call offer around \$2k–\$3k (guess) and a recurring community tier.

- **Regulators have tightened in 2026, so the content that drives sales in this niche is now a liability.** The UK FCA sentenced seven finfluencers in Feb 2026.\[1\] The FTC's Raging Bull order bans earnings claims unless they are typical.\[2\] Nigeria's SEC draft rules (1 Sep 2026, not yet in force) would ban lifestyle displays that imply trading wealth.\[3\] Jean's Mustang and payout-certificate content works against both his ads and his Nigerian audience.

## Recommendations

1.  **Build a high-ticket rung with an application and a call** (VIP plus 1:1, about \$2k–\$3k (guess)). Model the funnel on Nick Graves ("DM 'MENTOR'" → 2-minute application), but leave out the payout ticker.

2.  **Add a recurring community tier** at \$97–\$150/mo to match where Akram, Mentfx and Nick price. Keep \$399 as the entry course.

3.  **Mechanism:** sell "Prop-Firm Survival" (pass *and keep*, forex and NQ, with drawdown math). Back it with a third-party-verified track record and an affiliate-free firm review. Nobody else combines these.

4.  **Creative:** lead with process, losses and rule math. Retire the Mustang and the payout-certificate visuals from ads, and keep any payout content strictly educational with disclaimers.

5.  **Africa:** a WAT/EAT-timed London-session room and pricing for the Nigerian segment. Avoid broker affiliate links into Nigeria while the SEC draft is pending.
