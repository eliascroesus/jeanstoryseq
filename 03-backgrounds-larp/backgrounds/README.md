### Patterns

- **Overall look:** phone photos in natural or ambient light, no heavy editing (one B&W). Two moods: warm night interiors (amber lamps, candles, cognac leather, deep teal, black) and bright daytime (blue sky, cream, concrete). Cobalt blue, cream and black recur in both outfits and settings. Jean's styling is consistent: baggy jeans or long jorts, chains, white Nike socks, chunky sneakers or loafers, caps, a watch. Jean is usually looking down or covering their face, rarely straight to camera.
- **Recurring settings:** hotel and apartment lobbies, upscale restaurants, luxury boutiques, valet and supercars, night skylines, and a home trading desk in a high-rise apartment.
