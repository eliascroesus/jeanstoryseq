All files are `Screenshot 2026-11-10 at <time>.png`. The 12 screenshots are **one year-end sequence (H1)** from the same trading creator as the soft refs, selling their paid program, "the Syndicate". `11.42.27` and `11.42.47` are the **same frame** captured twice. Story order puts `11.42.32` before them.

### H1: "My year → I launched the program → student case studies → year ends in 24h → DM 'NEWYEAR'" (11 unique frames)

**Look:** every line sits in its own **white rounded box with black sans text** (Instagram's native text-with-background style), stacked and centred, sometimes running down half the frame. Emotion words are colour-tinted: **red/pink for pain** ("blowing", "never", "wasted", "wrong", "ends", "3 years"), **gold for reward** ("serious", "special", "NEWYEAR", "$" amounts, "FIRST", "proof"). Backgrounds are real photos and stills: a dim restaurant, a city at night, a car at a gas station, a hotel lobby, a car interior with purple ambient light. Proof is Discord screenshots on dark rounded cards, often with a Topstep payout certificate inside. Faces are blurred. A hand-drawn yellow arrow → prompts the next tap.
