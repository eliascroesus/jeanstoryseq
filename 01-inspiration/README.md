## Style breakdown across all references (so far)

Both reference sequences come from one creator in the same niche as Jean, trading (futures prop-firm payouts, Topstep, Discord community). They share a recognisable formula:

**Design**
- **It looks native, not designed.** Text is typed the way Instagram's own text tool does it: one short line per box, every box separate, stacked and centred. It doesn't look like a Canva graphic, and that's part of why it feels like a DM rather than an ad.
- There are two box styles: **white box + black sans** (hard sequence) and **black box + white italic serif** (soft sequence). Pick one per sequence. Closest web fonts are a clean geometric sans (Poppins / Outfit / Inter) for the white boxes, and an italic serif (Playfair Display / Libre Baskerville / Cormorant Garamond italic) for the black boxes.
- Colour does one job: a single word per line tinted **red/pink for pain** and **gold for money or reward**.
- **The background is always a real-life shot** (LARP): cars, night streets, restaurants, hotels, a car interior. Text sits on top with no overlay; the boxes make it readable.
- **Proof = Discord screenshots** on rounded dark cards, usually in the lower half, sometimes 2 side by side, sometimes a collage of many. Payout certificates are visible inside.
- Other people's faces are blurred. Students' first names are used in the copy.
- A small hand-drawn arrow nudges the tap-forward on the frame before the pitch.

**Copy**
- Short declarative lines, 3–7 per frame, lowercase-casual, with slang ("cook", "chopped", "locked in", "my boy").
- **Flex, then vulnerability**: a big number is immediately followed by losses, debt, or almost quitting.
- **"It's not just me"** is the bridge from the creator's story to the students.
- **Case-study formula:** *[Name] used to [specific trading pain]. [Low point]. And now? / Joined [program] [when]. [Exact $ result + timeframe]. [Meaning: "It was never about the money / proof this actually works"].* Then the screenshot.
- Pain is named in the audience's own words: "blowing accounts", "revenge trading", "cutting trades short", "knowing what you're doing wrong but still doing it".
- Soft CTA: give something free (video series) behind a **link sticker** to the free Discord.
- Hard CTA: empathy ("I've been there") → qualifier ("genuinely serious") → **time-boxed bonus** → **DM keyword** in quotes, highlighted.
